# Brag Plan: GatiSaarth

## What is this app?

GatiSaarth is a privacy-first, on-device navigation system that keeps a vehicle positioned when GNSS disappears, using only a phone's sensors and offline maps.

## The angle

Flagship launch film: the moment navigation normally goes blind, GatiSaarth keeps moving. Treat the product as calm, credible infrastructure—not a dashboard demo or a generic AI pitch.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

An evening route enters a tunnel. Satellite bars extinguish and the map does not freeze. Copy: “WHEN GNSS GOES DARK.”

## Key moments (the middle)

- A real app-inspired navigation view switches from blue GNSS trail to a dashed red dead-reckoning trail while the vehicle continues around a bend.
- The uncertainty ring expands honestly while the state reads “Dead Reckoning Active · GNSS Outage.”
- Offline vector roads remain legible; satellite reconnection resolves with a smooth, non-teleporting glide.

## Outro / punchline

The route emerges from the tunnel, the signal returns, and the GatiSaarth wordmark holds with: “INTELLIGENT NAVIGATION BEYOND GNSS.”

## User flow worth showing

Live route with GNSS lock → tunnel test / GNSS outage activates dead reckoning → GNSS returns and the route reconciles smoothly.

## Tone

- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Flagship launch video for appl
- Interpretation: Premium, restrained, trailer-scale confidence; large declarative type, dark map imagery, slow deliberate reveals and no hype-metric dump.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: #000000
- Accent: #0A84FF (GNSS), #FF3B30 (dead reckoning), #34C759 (reacquired)
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Display font: SF Pro Text / Inter fallback
- Body font: SF Pro Text / Inter fallback
- Strongest visual element: iOS-like dark navigation map with its honest expanding accuracy ring and blue-to-dashed-red route state change.

## Share copy (draft)

When GNSS disappears, GatiSaarth keeps the journey moving—on-device, offline, and built for the places navigation usually fails.

## Audio direction

- Role: cinematic support
- Music: restrained modern electronic pulse, `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app`; begins sparse, builds through outage, resolves warm at reacquisition.
- Music treatment: soft fade-in at 0s; modest level under UI moments; brief swell into the final wordmark and gentle tail-out.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset available; use a strong cue near the tunnel-entry hook, another for outage activation, and a final resolving cue for the logo. Detect exact timestamps in composition time. Sequential UI changes should land on every other beat and remain held.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the map glow and accuracy ring may breathe with the low-end energy.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint.
- Audio-coupled moments: satellite fade-out, two-pulse outage state, red route reveal, soft lock confirmation.
- Restraint rule: no alarm sirens, whooshes on every cut, or aggressive EDM drops.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Signal — 3.0s

Dark aerial-map-inspired field resolves into the real app's navigation treatment: blue route, moving vehicle puck, “GNSS LOCKED.” Large type settles: “WHEN GNSS GOES DARK.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — three satellite indicators extinguish one by one, then hold for reading.
Audio intent: low pulse and a subtle signal texture that drops away.
Audio-coupled idea: tiny lock ticks followed by a muted signal cut.
Music: sparse cinematic electronic opening.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Outage — 5.0s

The camera enters a tunnel-shaped mask over the actual navigation UI. “Dead Reckoning Active · GNSS Outage” replaces the lock state; the route changes from solid blue to dashed red while the puck continues through a curved road.
Sequential/interaction: yes — GNSS status fades, double haptic-style pulse lands, then the dashed route draws forward.
Audio intent: controlled tension, not panic.
Audio-coupled idea: two quiet pulses and a crisp route-state change.
Music: pulse gains weight beneath the map.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Honest navigation — 5.0s

The app-inspired UI fills the frame. The red trajectory keeps following the road; an uncertainty ring expands around the puck. Copy: “STILL ON THE ROAD.” Small supporting line: “On-device inertial navigation.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — ring expands, then labels settle and hold.
Audio intent: forward momentum and confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: restrained counter-like expansion texture, no literal beeps.
Music: steady, focused cinematic bed.
Transition mood: soft reveal → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Return — 4.0s

The tunnel opens. Green GNSS state returns, the red dashed route resolves back toward blue, and the puck glides into alignment instead of jumping. Copy: “NO FREEZE. NO TELEPORT.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — green lock appears before the route reconciliation; headline holds at least 1.2s.
Audio intent: release and reassurance.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet lock chime and a smooth resolving swell.
Music: warm lift into the final cue.
Transition mood: cinematic crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — GatiSaarth — 3.0s

Black field. GatiSaarth wordmark appears above “INTELLIGENT NAVIGATION BEYOND GNSS.” A thin blue route line travels across the lower third and comes to rest.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: decisive, premium end card.
Audio-coupled idea: one muted logo hit with a clean tail.
Music: final swell, then fade.
Transition mood: cut to black

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic electronic, calm propulsion.
**Audio summary:** A contained electronic pulse loses signal at the outage, holds tension while the route persists, then resolves into a clean lock-and-logo finish.
