Inside the navigation engine

Sensor fusion

A 15-state error-state Kalman filter combines inertial motion and accepted GNSS fixes. Stationary updates and vehicle motion constraints help manage accumulated error.

Edge intelligence

On-device TensorFlow Lite models contribute motion estimates. Freshness and confidence gates control how those estimates enter the navigation engine.

Offline road context

PMTiles map archives supply local road data. The app computes and saves routes for supported coverage, with journey planning currently limited to destinations under 80 km away.

Visible limits

Drift grows without reliable references. Phone mounting, sensor quality, and outage duration affect performance. Lane-level accuracy and unlimited outage navigation are not claimed.

Before you head out.

Does it work without internet?

Downloaded maps and saved journeys work offline. You’ll need a connection to download missing map coverage. Offline place search covers the maps already installed on your phone.

What happens when GPS is unavailable?

After an initial satellite fix, the app can estimate movement from the phone’s sensors. The uncertainty grows during an outage and is shown in the app. Long outages and poor mounting can make the position unreliable; follow road signs when confidence is low.

Do I need a special phone or vehicle sensor?

The APK supports Android 7.0 and newer with ARM or x86_64 processors. The navigation engine uses the phone’s available sensors. A stable phone mount matters; sensor availability and quality vary by device.
