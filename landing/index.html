Questions & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GatiSaarth need an active internet connection to navigate in tunnels?

No. GatiSaarth is 100% offline. Vector map tiles are bundled on-device using PMTiles v3, and the 15-state ES-EKF sensor fusion runs entirely in real-time on your phone's processor. You can run GatiSaarth in Airplane Mode with zero degradation.

Do I need expensive sensors or external hardware?

Zero external hardware is required. GatiSaarth utilizes the standard MEMS sensors already inside your Android smartphone (3-axis accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and barometer) through mathematical sensor fusion.

How does the phone know vehicle direction if it's placed sideways?

Our dynamic vehicle alignment algorithm estimates the mounting yaw and tilt angle automatically by correlating linear acceleration vectors during straight-line driving and braking maneuvers. Calibration completes within seconds.

Is my personal location data ever uploaded anywhere?

Never. GatiSaarth has no user tracking, no third-party advertising SDKs, and no telemetry servers. All sensor calculations, EKF states, and map rendering happen strictly in local RAM.
